# How Do You Test Whether AI Agents Are Ready for Production? With Ilan Kedar of Plurai

[[people/uri-eliabayev]] talks with [[people/ilan-kedar]], who founded Plurai after leading AI at Nexar, about getting agents from demo to production.
Plurai's simulator learns an agent and its spec and generates realistic scenarios to catch edge cases before users do, much as autonomous driving had to.
Synthetic data works only if it is diverse (task-dimension decomposition) and accurate (multi-agent debate labels kept only on consensus).
Production agents shift to an LLM planner plus many small task models: better accuracy, sub-100 ms latency, ~10× lower cost, and no vendor lock-in.
Next: continuous harness improvement, with traces, simulation and RL closing the loop.
