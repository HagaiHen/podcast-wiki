# Company Brain — with Roi Zalta (Microsoft)

[[people/roi-zalta]] walks the context timeline: RAG (2023) → agentic RAG → context engineering (2025) → Karpathy's Obsidian/LLM-wiki moment (April 2026) → the "company brain", shared organizational knowledge that agents consume and humans maintain.
RAG still covers ~99% of needs; a company brain isn't solved yet, mostly because keeping sources synced and contradictions resolved is expensive.
He proposes four layers (factual memory → human communication / "the missing why" → context-graph ontology → governed actions/loops), plus ontology "lenses" per role, authority weighting, and nightly "dreaming" jobs that consolidate memory.
Also: humans as knowledge nodes, agents and humans sharing workspaces and boards, and a hot take that SaaS is not dead.
