# #58 — AWS re:Invent 2026 Predictions — with Shuki Cohen (AI21)

Recorded in Las Vegas with [[people/shuki-cohen]] (VP Data at AI21, YouTube channel *One Shot Learning*), looking back at 2025 and ahead to 2026 across three layers.
**Models:** omni-modal models (Amazon Nova), often at the cost of reasoning; AWS Nova Forge, letting companies continue training from early checkpoints on their own data mix (like Thinking Machines' Tinker); niche expert models (latency, long context, task-specific); small encoder-only models for classification; and per-intent compute ("model choice") as the market starts caring about ROI.
**Infra:** speech-to-speech needs async reasoning delegation, async tool calling (Nova Sonic 2 keeps talking while tools run), and voice-specific evals; RL environments and reward endpoints; AWS AgentCore (memory, code runtime, browser, identity, policy); and MCP gateways.
**Apps:** orchestrators return. Multi-agent systems bring context isolation but also overlapping agents and stale capability descriptions. Autonomous agents are narrowing to niches.
Wishes for 2026: streaming omni-modal reasoning, and real memory management.
