# "Bloody Monday"? The Rule Nobody Told You About

[[people/micha-stocks]] answers viral posts predicting a Black-Monday-style crash: since 1988, circuit breakers halt the S&P 500 at −7% and −13% and close it at −20%, so a 1987-style −22.6% day can't recur for an index.
Even the 2020 COVID crash (−35%) took 23 trading days; only single stocks can collapse overnight.
The crash posts spread on extreme fear and are paid per view; that Monday the Nasdaq actually rose 1.2%.
Under the surface it was weak: equal-weight RSP fell and only chips and the Magnificent 7 lifted the index.
