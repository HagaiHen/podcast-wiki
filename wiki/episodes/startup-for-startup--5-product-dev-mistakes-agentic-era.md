# 5 Product–Engineering Mistakes in the Agentic Era

> Thin source: based on the episode description only.

A rebroadcast from "Productive", the network's product podcast, where [[people/ran-erez]] breaks down five common mistakes where product meets engineering in the agentic era.
Developers write more code than ever and PMs build prototypes or even ship to production, yet PMs still say they lack engineering resources.
Erez argues for bringing engineering evidence rather than conclusions, and for something better than a 70-page AI-written spec nobody reads.
He covers the pressure an "almost finished" prototype puts on engineering, creator vs owner, and evals that check how an answer looks rather than whether it helped.
Examples come from monday.com, Elementor and Stripe; the PM's key job is making sure the team solves the right problem.
