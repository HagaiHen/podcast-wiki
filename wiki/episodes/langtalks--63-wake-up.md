# #63 — WAKE UP

An unscripted, conceptual episode: the hosts feel "something is happening" (SaaS and security stocks falling, agents since around October 2025 running far longer and more reliably) and share predictions.
The new skill is managing *human* attention across parallel agents, as an "agent team lead". Most developers, especially in enterprises, are far from the frontier.
Unlike past shifts, intelligence itself becomes a commodity that scales with money, hardware, and energy. Open questions: what's left uniquely human (extrapolation beyond training data, creativity)? Will continuous learning arrive? Will autonomous businesses emerge (agents with crypto wallets paying for their own servers, hiring humans via "rent-a-human" services)?
Predictions from one host: about 5× productivity now, ~20× by end of 2026, ~100× in 2027, driven by background agents running overnight. Developers become orchestrators and environment engineers in the medium term, entry-level jobs get much harder, and deep CS fundamentals matter for the last 5%. Advice to R&D orgs: invest now in internal platforms, environments, and docs for agents.
