# The 9 Stages of AI Agent Adoption, Explained

[[people/greg-isenberg]] maps the "war on agents": Amazon blocks Meta's Muse, Delta refuses outside agents, Yelp charges, and Walmart and Zillow block by accident.
An agent is "a customer with a credit card and no eyes" that works on a ladder of trust (research, prepare, pay) and stays loyal to whatever worked last time.
Stores block because agents skip ads and upsells; he lays out each big player's bet and nine stages ending, as with Plaid and the banks, in a trusted identity-and-payments layer, the return of ads, and agent commerce as normal as a website.
This week: test 10 real buying questions with agents, check accidental bot blocking, put key facts in plain text, and gate only the pay rung.
Startup openings: per-industry agent readiness, a vertical Plaid, agent-mistake insurance, and agent-traffic analytics.
