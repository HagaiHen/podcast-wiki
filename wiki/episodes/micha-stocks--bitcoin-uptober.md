# Bitcoin Is Negative, but October Rose in 11 of 16 Years. An Opportunity?

[[people/micha-stocks]] looks at "Uptober": Bitcoin rose in October in 11 of 16 years (2010–2025), ~25% on average, yet October 2026 started negative for both Bitcoin (~$82k) and Ethereum (~$2,400).
Ethereum's October record is mixed and says nothing about its year; the assets "don't read the calendar", sentiment moves them.
Bitcoin's fall from ~$126k to ~$57k and rebound left it at its price of a year earlier.
He maps support and resistance levels ($82.8k, $87.4k, $74k invalidation; ETH $2,313 support, $2,807 breakout) and notes the ETH chart fits both bearish and bullish patterns.
Reminder: returns cluster in ~10 exceptional days a year and nobody knows which (opinion, not financial advice).
