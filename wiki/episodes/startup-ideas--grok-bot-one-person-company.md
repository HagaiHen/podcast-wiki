# Grok Bot: Make a One-Person Company with Agents

[[people/billy-howell]] shows how he runs a 6,000-subscriber local newsletter (the Arlington Bagel) with a GrokBot agent team that replaced an intern. GrokBot agents run on a cloud machine and are organized as DMs with distinct avatars, which forces mission focus. It costs ~$200–300 a month.
Method: pick *one* project (tokens and context bleed don't stretch across several). Start with a chief-of-staff agent that audits the business and proposes three revenue agents (research, sales, a platform expert). Have the chief of staff do a task once before spinning out a dedicated agent.
Four-week framework: build the team → execute with no tinkering → hire and fire agents → automate (routines with five-line end-of-day briefs to the chief of staff).
Practices: you make the decisions agents stall on; adversarial review panels of sub-agents; turn your feedback into a review skill; move deterministic steps (formatting blurbs) to cheap automations; keep research tasks closed-ended.
Business ideas: niche newsletters, AI-run Shopify stores, and SEO directories, which reinforce each other.
