# How a Combat Soldier With No Tech Background Built a Company That Sold for Over $600M | Amir Elichai

[[people/yasmin]] hosts [[people/amir-elichai]], an Egoz officer and founder-CEO of Carbyne, which Axon bought for $625M in cash plus ~$100M in equity.
Carbyne began when he called the police during a beach robbery and had to dictate his location; he validated the gap with police and emergency services and recruited two technical co-founders.
Fundraising stories: dozens of "no"s, a cold approach to Ehud Barak that became the first check, Robert Kraft and David Petraeus before product-market fit, and a SoftBank term sheet withdrawn a week after he'd turned down other funds.
The exit: announcing a $100M raise via Goldman Sachs to draw buyers, taking Axon in as lead investor, then six weeks from LOI to signing, with a "win-win sell point" instead of the peak.
Also: leadership from the army, brand and storytelling for government sales, business in the Emirates, AI agents and drones in 911, and why he'd have focused on the US from day one.
