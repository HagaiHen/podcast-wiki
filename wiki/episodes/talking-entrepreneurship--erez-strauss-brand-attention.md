# How to Capture Attention for Your Brand on a Minimal Budget, With Erez Strauss

[[people/erez-strauss]], who led creative at ExpressVPN (Kape Technologies) over the past year, explains how to win attention cheaply.
In a YouTube A/B test, cinematic, funny AI ads held viewers about twice as long as human UGC ads (19–25% vs 9–13% reaching 75% of the video). He calls this an "attention arbitrage" and walks through his pipeline from human insight to Claude shot list and Seedance clips to human editing.
His idea tools: an unexpected use, and a statistic shrunk to one person's tragedy. He pairs the AI scroll-stopper with remarketing that uses real people to build trust.
Cases: a World Cup "Big Ad Contest" for user-made AI ads with match tickets and a Times Square billboard, and a separate entertainment YouTube channel (privacy dating shows) that grew from 0 to 300k subscribers in months.
He and the host agree that creative ideas sound stupid until they work, so teams need tolerance and a 10–20% budget for experiments.
