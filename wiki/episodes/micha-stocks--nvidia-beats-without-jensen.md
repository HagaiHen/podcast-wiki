# Nvidia Without Jensen Beats Forecasts, and the Board Says He's Not Coming Back

[[people/micha-stocks]] covers Nvidia's first report since Jensen Huang's ouster. It beat on revenue and profit, raised guidance (data center $107B against ~$101B expected), and rose ~4% after hours, while the board said on the call that it won't bring Huang back.
Huang changed his X profile from CEO to "founder" and posted an apology. Micha wonders whether he might gather 15% of shareholders to call a special meeting and try to replace the board.
The rest of the market fell, with the Dow down 1.62% and Salesforce down ~9.5%. Software sold off on fears of an undisclosed cyber breach, after a week of outages at Microsoft, US banks, Apple Pay and Charles Schwab.
Morgan Stanley data: since October 1, defensive sectors rose 13% and the Nasdaq fell 13%. The 26% gap is the widest in 25 years, last seen in September–October 2001. Micha reads it as a broad rotation rather than an AI bubble bursting.
He also treats the flood of negativity in his comments as a sign of "peak disgust" and expects calm, and maybe a year-end rally, once the turmoil passes.
