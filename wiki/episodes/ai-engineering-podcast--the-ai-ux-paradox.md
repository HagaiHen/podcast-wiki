# The AI UX Paradox: AI Engineering Beyond the Chatbox — with Matan Cohen (Slack)

Host [[people/netanel-abergel]] opens with "Elnie", a personal agent with its own phone number, email, and accounts. It grew into a 30–40-agent WhatsApp network, which taught that chat is the wrong medium and that agents should be team members, not personal clones.
[[people/matan-cohen]] (co-founder of Dotti, acquired by Slack; now Head of Slack Israel) describes the AI UX paradox: a text box lets users ask anything and expect anything, yet hides what the product can actually do.
His answer is "proactive AI": surface capabilities through the product (a "prepare me for a meeting" button, AI answers in search) and bring answers to people where they already ask. Dotti's privately-shown channel auto-replies spread "like wildfire".
On measurement: from manual conversation review to intent clustering to offline evals in CI ("eval-driven design"), and the harder problem of finding unknown intents without seeing private user data.
Hot take: coding is dead, engineering isn't. Roles move up a level of abstraction, as network engineers became DevOps.
