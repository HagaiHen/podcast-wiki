# Special Economic Interview: Naftali Bennett Commits to One Number, Checkable in a Year

[[people/micha-stocks]] interviews former PM [[people/naftali-bennett]] about his party's economic plan before the elections (campaign claims, not verified).
Bennett's core move: redirect ~₪30B in benefits from people who neither work nor serve to those who do, e.g. ₪4,000/month daycare subsidies for active reservists, to raise draft and workforce participation.
On cost of living he blames stalled competition rather than firms, and rejects price caps because they cause shortages; the host presses on subsidies being captured as higher prices.
On AI: sovereign AI for defense on open-source models in isolated data centers, a "red carpet" for data centers, an AI agent for every citizen with APIs to government services, and Israel as a defense-tech hub.
His checkable pledge: daycare costs (ages 0–3) for working, reserve-serving couples down 80% within a year.
