# #75 — Post-Training, With Dan Padnos of Novee

[[people/dan-padnos]], Head of AI at Novee Security, explains how vertical AI companies train open-weight models on their own tasks, and separates pre-, mid- and post-training.
Supervised fine-tuning learns from a static dataset; reinforcement learning scores rollouts and is engineered much like agent evaluation at scale.
A host plays devil's advocate: harness, knowledge and memory offer easier wins, and owning a model means continual retraining. Padnos agrees, yet calls it a strategic R&D path that matures later, as at Cursor and Harvey.
Practical advice: start with a small, established model, overfit one example, and use Unsloth or Axolotl for SFT. RL has fewer easy tools.
Rewards can include errors and episode length, credit assignment stays open, and simulated environments show which layer to fix first.
