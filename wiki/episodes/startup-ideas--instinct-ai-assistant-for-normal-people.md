# Instinct AI: The AI Assistant for Normal People

[[people/greg-isenberg]] and [[people/remy]] (AI with Remy) tour Instinct AI, an invite-only personal agent that lives in iMessage and WhatsApp and is rumored to be raising at $10B.
Remy's real errands: a haircut booked by email when online booking needed a Danish number, a restaurant table, an airline loyalty sign-up, and a Bali visa on arrival done from a passport photo.
What works: one-step onboarding, native iMessage reactions and effects, short replies, memory across tasks, proactive nudges, its own email inbox, and fast hidden computer use. It's "OpenClaw/Hermes for regular people."
Concerns: reports of retained emails after disconnecting, data terms, and silent stretches that look like failure. Remy contains spending with a limited virtual card.
Business lessons: invite-only scarcity as marketing (Clubhouse comparison) and a trusted-person network as agent-era network effects.
