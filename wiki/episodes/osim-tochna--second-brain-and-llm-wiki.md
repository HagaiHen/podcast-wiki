# Second Brain & LLM Wiki — with Dana Maman

[[people/amit-bendor]] talks with [[people/dana-maman]], a non-developer who builds apps with Claude, about her "second brain".
She rebuilt her notes on [[people/andrej-karpathy]]'s LLM Wiki pattern (raw / wiki / schema), then grew it into a personal "operating system" holding meetings, projects, apps, design system, and writing.
The core lessons are about trust: instructions aren't enough, so enforce rules with hooks and loops, verify facts and links by reading sources, and investigate every failure.
They also cover file structure, knowledge rot, retrieval beyond ~100 sources (multiple query guesses, ranking candidates, RAG/hybrid), and Google's OKF format.
Getting started: let the AI interview you, sketch your life domains on paper, start simple and restructure freely.
