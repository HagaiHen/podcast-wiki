# Breaking: Nvidia

A breaking-news update from [[people/micha-stocks]]: the New York Times reports that Jensen Huang had a relationship with a senior Nvidia executive who reported to him, days after an earlier story about a suspected Chinese spy, and the board may fire him. Nvidia falls ~7.8% after hours, below $160.
He frames it as a continuity problem (the Steve Jobs comparison only partly fits), then plays the day's planned video.
November 11 recap: the AI trade returns for a day (Nasdaq +2.29%, broad participation via RSP), CoreWeave jumps 25% after its CEO cancels automatic share sales, and oil stays ~$85 on Iran-deal hopes.
Nine analysts revise Nvidia within two days and Morgan Stanley drops it from its top picks, but most still rate it buy, so capitulation isn't complete.
Overlaying Nvidia's chart on Google's shows the same bouncing pattern and the same ~40% gap to all-time highs, suggesting the drop may be an ordinary correction with a story attached.
