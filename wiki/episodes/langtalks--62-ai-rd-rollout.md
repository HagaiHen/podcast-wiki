# #62 — AI R&D Rollout — with Iko Azoulay (Salt Security)

[[people/iko-azoulay]] (EVP Technology at Salt Security) recounts rolling AI out across R&D. It started with GitHub Copilot licenses (and arguments with the CFO), moved through a three-month "accelerator" trying every tool, and settled mainly on Claude Code for its codebase understanding and plan mode. Copilot stays for tab completion as a "safety belt".
Adoption levers: a GenAI guild of early adopters sharing success stories and workshops, and a challenge to spend a sprint writing *no* code by hand. The mindset shift is the real hurdle.
The flow: a detailed spec as a *contract* between human, machine, and product; tech design grounded in repo context; task breakdown; AI validating AI at every step, while humans stay accountable. Next steps: product creating working-mock PRs on real data, hierarchical cross-repo context, and less rigid, more interactive artifacts.
Big picture: "alignment engineering" may be next year's hype; task lengths agents handle are growing exponentially; Scrum rituals will have to change.
