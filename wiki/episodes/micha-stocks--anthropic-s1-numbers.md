# Anthropic's Numbers Are Out, and They Should Scare You

[[people/micha-stocks]] goes through Anthropic's S-1 filing (ticker ANT): H1 2026 revenue of $16.3B against $1.1B a year earlier, with 1,900+ customers paying over $1M a year.
Losses, mostly compute, are huge. At a $1.5T valuation the multiple is ~327× 2025 revenue, and ~$270B in cloud contracts with Google, Amazon and Microsoft is already committed.
Anthropic's pitch ties commercial success to humanity's survival and to a closed train-sell-compute loop; Micha calls much of the trillion-dollar projections fantasy.
Elon Musk warned that credit markets have closed to neoclouds, which hit the group at the open before investors started picking winners and losers. Micha notes Musk's own agenda.
Also: a surprise Brazilian election result sent its index down ~20% in a day.
