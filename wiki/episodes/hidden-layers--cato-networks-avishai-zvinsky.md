# How Do You Handle the New AI-Driven Cyber Threats? — with Avishai Zvinsky of Cato Networks

[[people/uri-eliabayev]] talks with [[people/avishai-zvinsky]], who leads Cato Networks' security-content group (attack protection, traffic classification, third-party integrations).
Agentic coding spread from one enthusiast with a POC; the team seeded shared rules and skills from infra code and Confluence, and agents now write their own skills after sessions.
The CVE-to-protection pipeline (triage, lab exploit, signature, test on historical traffic, silent mode, block) went from half a day to as little as 45 minutes as an agentic workflow, built entirely as natural-language rules and skills.
Agentic attackers are real but human-driven and error-prone; protocols don't change, but pace does, so 12-hour response windows are gone.
Cato runs an agentic attack lab on Kali Linux and is building behavioral and in-line agentic defenses.
