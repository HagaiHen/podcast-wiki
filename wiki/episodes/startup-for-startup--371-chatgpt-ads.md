# #371 — Advertising in ChatGPT: Building a Marketing Channel With No Playbook

> Host: Roni Arniv (Startup for Startup).

[[people/yoav-rubin]] and [[people/tamar-hazoli]], growth marketers at monday.com, describe advertising in ChatGPT since the first day of ads (February 2026), as one of the first ~100 advertisers.
ChatGPT sits between push and pull. Targeting runs through ~10-word context hints but mostly through the ad's content, so campaigns are split by intent (Tool Seekers, Chat Actions, Solution Seekers) with many varied ads and content-heavy landing pages.
What worked: OCPC event bidding, action-verb ads, high contrast and product close-ups. Google-style ads didn't. CPC is about a quarter of Google's because competition is still low.
The pain is visibility: no data on prompts or users, ads shown only to logged-out, free and low-tier users, and click attribution that undercounts, so they add view-through and geo tests.
Sponsored agents inside the chat come next. Advice: send conversion events, design campaigns by intent, run many ads, and measure cross-channel lift.
