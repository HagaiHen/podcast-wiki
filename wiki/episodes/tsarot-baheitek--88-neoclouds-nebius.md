# #88 Neoclouds, GPU Data Centers, and a Thousand GPUs From the State, With Ido From Nebius

[[people/ido]], a technical partner solutions manager at Nebius, explains neoclouds: GPU-dedicated clouds from bare-metal resellers to full "hyperscalers of AI".
Nebius is vertically integrated, from building data centers (GPU racks draw 55–140+ kW) to its own servers and virtualization layer.
He describes the Israel Innovation Authority tender: 1,000 subsidized GPUs at the Modiin site, where the real value is reserved capacity.
Why companies move from closed frontier APIs to self-deployed open models: cost, model differentiation, sovereignty; inference in production is a deep specialty.
Also: the shift from training to inference, inference-only data centers near customers, and in-demand data-center and AI-transformation jobs.
