# 3 Stages of Adopting AI: From First Adoption to Rebuilding Processes

> Host: Roni Arniv (Startup for Startup).

[[people/dror-adi]], AI Innovation Lead in monday.com's finance department, explains how a regulated, globally scattered department moved through three stages of AI adoption.
Stage one drove usage toward 100% with "#BlameDror" reassurance, read-only connections, a team car-race dashboard and an internal AI pioneers program.
Stage two built many agents on existing processes, starting with low-effort work outside SOX scope and iterating through good/bad feedback from the teams.
A department leader's "where is this going?" and an industrial-revolution analogy convinced him that hours saved isn't a strategy, so stage three rebuilds one area at a time.
Collections came first: one agent gathers data from about eight platforms into a prioritized, personalized monday view, and it was nearing global launch at recording time.
