# #12 How Did Nas Daily Reach 450 Million Views With 100 AI Videos?

[[people/nuseir-yassin]] (Nas Daily) explains his 100-videos-in-100-days AI series, made when Veo 3 launched: about 450M views, with AI videos outperforming his filmed ones.
AI let him make videos that couldn't be filmed: history of places, proverbs and children's stories.
Pipeline: ChatGPT writes ~70% of the script, he records the voiceover, editors generate in Runway, Kling, Veo 3 and Seedance and spend ~48 hours per minute in Final Cut Pro. Credits fell from $500–1,000 to ~$200 per video, ~$100k in total, with almost no direct revenue.
He also pitches Nas.io as "Shopify for the age of AI" for solopreneurs, recalls learning performance marketing from the host, and shows a Gemini + Nano Banana infographic trick.
Much of the transcript is garbled (mixed Hebrew/English), so figures are approximate.
