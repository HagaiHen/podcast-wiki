# Meta, Tesla, Netflix and More Report, and the Stock I Forgot Turns Around

[[people/micha-stocks]] previews a heavy earnings week (GE Aerospace, Netflix, Intuitive Surgical, Tesla, Meta, IBM, Lam Research, Texas Instruments, Spotify, defense names).
The prior week the Nasdaq fell 5% and chips 12%, yet most S&P 500 sectors (staples, energy, health care, materials, utilities) rose.
Themes to listen for: Meta's AI capex, Lam Research's orders, Netflix and Spotify pricing power and subscriptions, defense budgets, Tesla's autonomy story.
For each company: expected move and how the market reacted to recent reports (Meta and Netflix mostly sold off).
His checklist: EPS vs revenue vs guidance, margins, call tone, then wait for the market's reaction; beating isn't enough without a story.
