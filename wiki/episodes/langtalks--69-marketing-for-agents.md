# #69 — Marketing for Agents — with Nevo David (Postiz)

[[people/nevo-david]], solo founder of Postiz (social-media scheduling), says making it agent-ready took revenue from $21k to $103k a month in two months, with no VC and no employees.
How: expose the product as a *skill* (an MD file built from the API docs) for personal agents and as an *MCP* for remote agents like ChatGPT and Claude. Then get discovered via GitHub "awesome" lists (SEO/AEO), ClawHub, and the official ChatGPT/Claude marketplaces; Claude listings (1.2M downloads) lead to Claude citing Postiz.
CLI versus MCP: CLIs are lighter, faster, and keep credentials out of the model's context (env vars or OAuth login); MCPs carry heavy JSON context but are needed for ChatGPT and stateful cases. Build both, and design CLIs with good help and gradual exposure.
The bigger picture: agents increasingly pick and embed vendors (you add the credit card later), and consumers make decisions through ChatGPT. Agent-ready products capture new demand that most SaaS still ignores.
