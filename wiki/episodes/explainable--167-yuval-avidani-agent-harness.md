# #167 The AI Harness and the Power of Persistence: Yuval Avidani on Agent Harnesses, the Future of Development, and Not Giving Up

[[people/mike-erlihson]] hosts builder and speaker [[people/yuval-avidani]], who explains a harness as the managed wrapper around an agent's model, tools, memory and skills.
He demos Amazon Bedrock AgentCore Harness live: a Haiku 4.5 math agent with a code interpreter, built in a few clicks, with one-click model swaps and session IDs.
They discuss which professions AI takes next (data science, QA, research, design, video) and show local and one-prompt video generation.
On lab economics: $20 subscriptions can't cover the compute, labs publish no financials, and profitability forecasts slipped to 2029.
His advice for building a name: create consistently about what genuinely interests you and show the how, even with zero likes.
