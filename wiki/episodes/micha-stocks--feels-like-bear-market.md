# Let's Talk About the Situation: It Feels Like a Bear Market

[[people/micha-stocks]] starts with a reported $6.3B Binance hack, which pushed Bitcoin to about $77k, and asks whether to hold coins directly or through ETFs.
He then walks through the Magnificent 7: the S&P 500 and Nasdaq are up for the year, yet several giants and the chip sector sit deep in drawdowns.
October was a rotation rather than a broad decline: equal-weight, value and nine sectors rose while chips, communication services and tech fell.
He argues roughly $4T has left the giants, that their results don't justify bear-market prices, and that heavy infrastructure spending is what the market dislikes.
He calls it a great opportunity, while stressing that bottoms can't be timed, prices can fall further, and this isn't financial advice.
