# The Nasdaq Enters a Bear Market: Is This the Time to Buy?

[[people/micha-stocks]] covers November 20, 2026: the Nasdaq fell 5.47% to 20% below its high, turning negative for the year, with Nvidia −13.8%, AMD and Micron −13% and SanDisk −16.3%.
Nvidia has lost ~48% (~$2.8T) since early October, after a Reuters report of an SEC accounting review and Gavin Baker's warning that competitors are forcing Nvidia to offer better terms.
Trump said American providers won't be allowed to build data centers abroad without a heavy tax; Nebius fell 16% and IREN is 84% below its high. Bitcoin sat near $58k (−33% YTD).
The VIX hit 40 and Fear & Greed ended at 10; Micha can't call the bottom but bought the Nasdaq for the ~25% upside back to its high and wants a strong green week as confirmation.
His rules for staying in: stops, a long horizon, the S&P 500 for most people, and zero leverage.
