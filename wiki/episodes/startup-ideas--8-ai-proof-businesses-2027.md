# 8 AI-Proof Businesses I'd Build in 2027

[[people/greg-isenberg]] asks what still holds value when AI makes software, content and answers nearly free.
His eight categories: atoms (physical businesses), permission (licenses, regulation, preferred-vendor deals), attention (AI-amplified media), and timing (first mover on niche trends, keeping the data and trust).
Then belonging (paid communities and IRL events, like Sam Parr's Hampton), liquidity (niche marketplaces run by tiny agent-assisted teams), and unique data (selling to labs or curated directories).
Last comes outcomes (automated agent services), which he ranks weakest; physical and license businesses rank strongest.
Mix categories instead of picking just one.
