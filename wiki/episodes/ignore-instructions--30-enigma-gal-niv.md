# #30 Building the Brain of Robots, With Gal Niv of Enigma

[[people/gal-niv]], CTO and co-founder of Enigma, describes building "a ChatGPT for robotics": software that can run on any robot and make it smart. Enigma recently ran a live "Robots Online" event where people remotely controlled a drawing robot.
His path went from cybersecurity to interpretability research, which he found always trailed the models by months, then to world models. His team's two-player Gran Turismo world model went viral and led to the company.
He separates classical robotics, task-specific learned skills and general foundation models. Enigma's first customers are large enterprises with labor shortages, and the company bets on models that generalize to unseen environments and tasks.
On the market: Unitree's open SDK as the "Android" play versus vertical humanoid makers; most real deployments are arms and quadrupeds; dexterous hands exist but aren't production-ready.
On safety, he sees readable chains of thought and the timing of the debate, before robots are widely deployed, as bright spots.
