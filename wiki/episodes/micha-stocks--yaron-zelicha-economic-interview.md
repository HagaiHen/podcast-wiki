# Special Economic Interview: Prof. Yaron Zelicha. Will He Really Lower the Cost of Living?

[[people/micha-stocks]] interviews economist [[people/yaron-zelicha]], former Accountant General, running with the Economic Party in the 2026 Israeli election.
Zelicha's plan targets import barriers: abolish tariffs and quotas, bar local monopolies from importing, split import cartels, and enforce competition law; he claims Israelis overpay ~35%.
He'd cut VAT to 12% via growth, and argues housing booms when interest rates lag growth; Micha counters that more disposable income feeds inflation and housing.
Much of the interview is a combative exchange over his 2003 role, past disputes, and consulting clients.
He also predicts sovereign digital currencies but no Bitcoin takeover, and opposes abolishing cash.
