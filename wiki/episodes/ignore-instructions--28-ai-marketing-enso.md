# #28 — How AI Is Changing Marketing — with Micky Haslavsky (enso)

> Transcript gap: a Whisper repetition loop in the middle (during the Ray Dalio outreach story) lost some content.

[[people/micky-haslavsky]] (enso) argues that agents didn't automate old marketing; they created a *new* kind: "agentic growth hacking" in places humans can't scale to, like reading ten subreddits all day, filling Wikipedia gaps, or exploiting LinkedIn's first-60-minutes ranking window.
He separates scripted *agentic workflows* (99% of production agents) from *autonomous agents* that learn by iterating, a "thinking multiplier". His example: an OpenClaw agent with email, browser, and phone tools found a way to reach Ray Dalio for his podcast after about nine attempts.
What works today: organic and unconventional channels, SEO/GEO, SDRs with modest context, and hyper-personalization ("1,000 go-to-markets", per-person newsletters). Paid ads don't work yet. enso pairs Forward Deployed Marketers with hundreds of agents.
The host shares an AI copywriting setup (GPT 5.6 as the best copywriter; memory.md; interview first, distill lessons after); Haslavsky's advice is to feed it real performance data.
