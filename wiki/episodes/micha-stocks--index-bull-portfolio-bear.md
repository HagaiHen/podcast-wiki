# The Index Says Bull Market, Your Portfolio Says Bear. Who's Right?

[[people/micha-stocks]] explains why the S&P 500 sits within 5% of its high while most portfolios feel like a bear market: 84% of its stocks are 10%+ off their highs and 59% are 20%+ off.
Ten giants (Nvidia, Micron, Apple, Microsoft, AMD, Alphabet…) delivered ~70% of the gain; 7 of 11 sectors are down and equal-weight RSP badly lags the cap-weighted index.
He argues concentration is a fact but not a reason to avoid the index, and that beaten-down stocks are not automatically bargains.
On valuation: earnings grew far faster than prices, so the P/E fell to ~18.9 and tech carries zero premium over the market, unlike 2000; but a ~5% Treasury yield matches the market's earnings yield, keeping it range-bound.
What to watch: RSP vs S&P 500, Brent, the 10-year yield, and the top ten companies; trade less while the market is stuck (opinion, not financial advice).
