# Startups

Building and running a startup: raising money (rejections, parallel conversations, term sheets that fall through), leading as a founder-CEO, measuring outcomes rather than inputs, and choosing when to sell. Also defense and public-safety tech, where Israel's battle experience and government buyers shape what gets built.

## Concepts
- [[concepts/fundraising]]: most pitches are "no"; run in parallel; a term sheet isn't money
- [[concepts/founder-leadership]]: the CEO is always selling; officers' leadership; focus on one market
- [[concepts/startup-exits]]: each raise removes buyers; win-win sell point; the acquirer as investor
- [[concepts/outcome-metrics]]: outcome KPIs over inputs; Goodhart's law; north-star metrics
- [[concepts/defense-tech]]: government as design partner; AI and drones in 911 and public safety
- [[concepts/attention-marketing]]: unexpected uses, one person's story, contests, entertainment channels, room to experiment
- [[concepts/ai-video-ads]]: cinematic AI ads as attention arbitrage; human insight and taste; remarket with real people
