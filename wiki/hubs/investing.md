# Investing

Personal investing: how to build wealth slowly with broad index funds, and the traps (leverage, concentration, social-media FOMO) that wipe people out.

## Concepts
- [[concepts/index-investing]]: buy-and-hold broad indexes; learn the basics first
- [[concepts/compound-interest]]: slow wealth by starting early
- [[concepts/leverage]]: borrowed money multiplies losses and takes away your exit, via margin calls or x2–x5 funds that can go to zero
- [[concepts/concentration-risk]]: when an "index" is really 2–3 stocks
- [[concepts/survivorship-bias]]: you only see the winners
- [[concepts/fomo]]: others' apparent gains push you into risk
