# Technical Analysis (moving averages, support and resistance)

**Summary:** Technical analysis reads price charts rather than business fundamentals. A trader's basic toolkit: where the price sits relative to its 50- and 150-day moving averages, support levels the price keeps bouncing off, resistance levels it fails to break, trend lines, volume that confirms a breakdown, and named patterns (cup and handle, head and shoulders, triple bottom). The value is in defining in advance which price confirms a thesis and which invalidates it. Charts can also strip away the story: overlaid on a stock with no news at all, a "scandal" selloff can look like the same ordinary correction. The weakness is ambiguity: the same chart can honestly be read as two opposite patterns.

## Key ideas
- Moving averages as a trend filter: below both the 50- and 150-day averages is a "falling knife" until proven otherwise (Netflix before earnings); crossing back above both is classically bullish (Intuitive Surgical) ([[episodes/micha-stocks--earnings-week-meta-tesla-netflix]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- Nasdaq broke below its 50-day average while staying ~2% above its 150-day, a spot he wouldn't call a good entry yet ([[episodes/micha-stocks--earnings-week-meta-tesla-netflix]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- Support is where the price keeps bouncing without breaking (Ethereum ~$2,313–2,350 since August); a series of higher lows (1,500 → 1,800 → 2,300) is positive ([[episodes/micha-stocks--bitcoin-uptober]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- Resistance comes from earlier highs: Bitcoin's ~$82.8k was the high before a May drop; breaking it is a "first indication", not a guarantee ([[episodes/micha-stocks--bitcoin-uptober]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- Set the invalidation level too: below ~$74k "we probably haven't found the bottom" ([[episodes/micha-stocks--bitcoin-uptober]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- A chart at a decision point can resolve either way: Lam Research was forming a possible triple bottom or about to break down ([[episodes/micha-stocks--earnings-week-meta-tesla-netflix]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- He doesn't buy right before an earnings report, even when the chart looks great ([[episodes/micha-stocks--earnings-week-meta-tesla-netflix]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- Trend lines: Nvidia bounced off its trend line with a bullish candle, "let's hope it lasts more than a day" ([[episodes/micha-stocks--anthropic-s1-numbers]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- Support and resistance on an index: QQQ bounced from support around 700 after a Friday doji, with resistance expected around 731 ([[episodes/micha-stocks--fed-rate-hike-odds]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- Volume confirms a breakdown: Boeing had already broken its line, and its 10.6% drop on heavy volume showed investors "throwing the stock down the stairs"; it trades where it was in 2020 ([[episodes/micha-stocks--fed-rate-hike-odds]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- Support under news pressure: Nvidia reached support around $162–166 and kept bouncing there, with the question being whether it holds above ~$162 ([[episodes/micha-stocks--breaking-nvidia-ceo]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- Read the chart without the news: overlay a stock on a peer with no news (Nvidia vs Google year to date, on a separate price scale) and see whether the pattern differs. Both were bouncing on support and both sat about 40% below their all-time highs, which suggests the "scandal" is only the explanation attached to a correction ([[episodes/micha-stocks--breaking-nvidia-ceo]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).

## Disagreements & open questions
- Pattern ambiguity: the same Ethereum chart reads as a head and shoulders (bearish) or a tilted cup and handle (bullish). "Everything is possible right now" ([[episodes/micha-stocks--bitcoin-uptober]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).

## Takeaways
- [ ] Before entering a trade, write down both the confirming price and the price that would prove you wrong ([[episodes/micha-stocks--bitcoin-uptober]])
- [ ] When a stock falls on news, overlay its chart on a similar stock without that news before deciding the news is the cause ([[episodes/micha-stocks--breaking-nvidia-ceo]])
