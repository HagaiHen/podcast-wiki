# Knowledge Rot

**Summary:** Knowledge bases decay: completed projects keep resurfacing, abandoned plans show up as "50 days late", and clipped files never get processed. The AI only knows what you tell it, so a living system needs routines that check freshness and loops that check whether the routines themselves still run, produce output, and are still wanted.

## Key ideas
- Dashboards kept nagging about a project she had dropped and never told the system about ([[episodes/osim-tochna--second-brain-and-llm-wiki]], [[people/dana-maman]]).
- Clipped files sat in the inbox unprocessed because no rule said to ingest from there; then a routine ran but she'd put files elsewhere. Check once, then 800 more files flow fine ([[episodes/osim-tochna--second-brain-and-llm-wiki]]).
- Routine health ladder: installed → runs → produces output → produces what I want, to my taste → do I still need it? (an idea-hunting job ran for days unread) ([[episodes/osim-tochna--second-brain-and-llm-wiki]]).
- Without a processing routine, raw captures "rot in the warehouse" ([[episodes/osim-tochna--second-brain-and-llm-wiki]]).
