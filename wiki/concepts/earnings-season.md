# Earnings Season (reading quarterly reports)

**Summary:** Each quarter, companies report results before the open or after the close, and the stock's reaction often matters more than the numbers. Read earnings per share against revenue and against guidance for the next quarter, then margins and the tone of the call, and wait for the market's verdict before acting. Beating estimates isn't enough; history is full of beats followed by drops, so a company also needs a convincing story. Options markets price an "expected move" for each report, and a company's past reactions show how the market treats it. Earnings season is also a cheap way to learn: with AI tools, digesting a CEO's call takes minutes.

## Key ideas
- Checklist: EPS vs revenue vs guidance; margins; what management says on the call and its tone; then wait for the market's reaction ([[episodes/micha-stocks--earnings-week-meta-tesla-netflix]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- Beating estimates isn't enough: many companies beat and the stock still fell; "you need to tell an interesting story" ([[episodes/micha-stocks--earnings-week-meta-tesla-netflix]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- Expected move and track record: Meta was priced for ~9.5% and fell 7.9–11% on most recent reports; Netflix had four red reactions in a row (expected ~7.3%); Tesla ~6%; Intuitive Surgical ~8% with swings both ways ([[episodes/micha-stocks--earnings-week-meta-tesla-netflix]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- Read reports for themes, not just the company: Meta's capex and how much free cash flow goes to AI infrastructure (it can scare or reassure the market); Lam Research's orders, i.e. how much is being invested in chip equipment; Netflix and Spotify for pricing power and whether consumers cut subscriptions; defense companies for military budgets ([[episodes/micha-stocks--earnings-week-meta-tesla-netflix]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- For "story" companies the narrative is the report: if Tesla is an autonomous-driving company, FSD miles, regulation, and Cybercab traction matter more than deliveries ([[episodes/micha-stocks--earnings-week-meta-tesla-netflix]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- Learning routine: each week pick one or two reporting companies; download the call transcript, slides, and earnings release (free on Perplexity Finance), upload them to NotebookLM, and ask for a simple summary or a deck focused on what the CEO said. After a 5–6 week season you understand ~12 companies ([[episodes/micha-stocks--bloody-monday-circuit-breakers]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
