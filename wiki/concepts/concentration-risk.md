# Concentration Risk

**Summary:** An "index" stops being diversified when a few stocks dominate it. In 2026 Samsung and SK Hynix grew to about half of the KOSPI (usually closer to a quarter for its top names) only because they were the stocks retail investors could leverage. When they fell, the whole index fell with them. Know what is inside what you buy.

## Key ideas
- Concentration is never good; the KOSPI effectively became a 2–3 stock index ([[episodes/lazy-investing--108-the-leverage-trap]], [[people/tamir]]).
- Israel's TA-35 caps any single stock at 7%, a rule introduced after Teva grew to dominate the index ([[episodes/lazy-investing--108-the-leverage-trap]]).
