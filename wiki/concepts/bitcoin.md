# Bitcoin (and crypto as an asset)

**Summary:** Bitcoin is an extremely volatile asset: 70–80% drawdowns happen, and in 2025–2026 it fell from ~$126k to ~$57k and rebounded to ~$82k, roughly where it stood a year earlier. Its October record is strong ("Uptober"), but the price doesn't read the calendar; moves come from sentiment, and October says even less about Ethereum. On the macro side, a former Israeli Accountant General expects states to issue their own digital currencies and doesn't think Bitcoin or any private coin will displace sovereign money, because governments won't give up seigniorage. Both speakers frame crypto as a high-risk asset, not something ordinary savers need.

## Key ideas
- Bitcoin rose ~126.2k → fell to ~57k → ~82k by mid-October 2026, about its November 2025 price: "a year of doing nothing", which a buy-and-hold investor who ignores volatility shrugs off ([[episodes/micha-stocks--bitcoin-uptober]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- October 2026 broke the pattern: both Bitcoin (~$82k) and Ethereum (~$2,400) were negative month-to-date and year-to-date (Bitcoin −6% YTD) ([[episodes/micha-stocks--bitcoin-uptober]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- Ethereum tends to move with Bitcoin but more wildly (+8,900% in one year, then a terrible 2018), so Ethereum can be down even in a year Bitcoin is up ([[episodes/micha-stocks--bitcoin-uptober]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- Levels he watched: above ~$82.8k as a first sign of recovery, ~$87.4k resistance (then $92k and $108k), and a break below ~$74k meaning the bottom isn't in. For Ethereum: support ~$2,313–2,350, breakout ~$2,807 ([[episodes/micha-stocks--bitcoin-uptober]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- Sovereign digital currencies (a digital shekel, a digital dollar) are coming, but private coins won't become dominant rivals: each state profits from seigniorage, and the US earns it globally from the dollar as reserve currency, so it won't let Bitcoin replace it ([[episodes/micha-stocks--yaron-zelicha-economic-interview]], [[people/yaron-zelicha]]).
- Private coins will still exist as competition among payment methods and a driver of technology ([[episodes/micha-stocks--yaron-zelicha-economic-interview]], [[people/yaron-zelicha]]).
- Zelicha holds no crypto and calls it a "roller coaster": very risky and volatile, for experts rather than ordinary citizens ([[episodes/micha-stocks--yaron-zelicha-economic-interview]], [[people/yaron-zelicha]]).
- Related stance: he opposes abolishing cash, both for privacy and because more payment methods mean more competition; a cashless economy is "the banks' dream" of fees on every payment ([[episodes/micha-stocks--yaron-zelicha-economic-interview]], [[people/yaron-zelicha]]).

## Disagreements & open questions
- Opportunity or warning? Micha reads a weak October against a 68%+ positive record as possible upside ("I'm for Uptober"); the same data also fits "these assets don't follow the calendar" ([[episodes/micha-stocks--bitcoin-uptober]]). See [[concepts/seasonality]].
