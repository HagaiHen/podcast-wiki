# Interest-Rate Expectations (market-implied Fed odds)

**Summary:** Before each Fed meeting, futures markets price the probability of each rate outcome, so a decision is usually known in advance and the surprise, if any, is in the press conference and the following meeting. In October 2026 markets gave a 92% chance of no change, but ~84% odds of a hike at the December meeting. The Fed avoids acting a week before US midterm elections so as not to look political, and the market prices that delay too. For traders, the levels to watch around such decisions are the 10-year Treasury yield (5% as the line) and oil (around $100), either of which can free the market up or weigh on it.

## Key ideas
- Read the odds, not the headlines: 92% hold and ~7% change for the October meeting; for December 9, 78% for a 25 bp hike plus 5.9% for a larger one, ~84% in total ([[episodes/micha-stocks--fed-rate-hike-odds]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- Politics in the timing: the Fed won't hike a week before the midterms so as not to look political, which means the timing itself is political, while a hike is still coming ([[episodes/micha-stocks--fed-rate-hike-odds]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- Expect two-sided language: worried about inflation picking up but seeing signs it's under control, worried about jobs weakening but seeing a strong labor market ([[episodes/micha-stocks--fed-rate-hike-odds]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- The market runs ahead of the Fed: two up days into the decision, but recent statistics show a red day after Fed meetings, so don't get excited about the green ([[episodes/micha-stocks--fed-rate-hike-odds]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- Watch the 10-year yield (just under 5%, "as long as it's under 5% we're calm") and oil (flirting with $100) as the two keys to the market's direction ([[episodes/micha-stocks--fed-rate-hike-odds]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
