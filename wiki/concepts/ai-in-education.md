# AI in Education

**Summary:** The proposed model: a personal AI tutor for every child that crosses the curriculum and exams with the child's specific level, so instruction reaches each student, while the teacher's role shifts to mentor and educator (character, values, field trips), the parts AI doesn't cover. Alpha School takes this to the edge: about two hours a day of AI-driven learning and the rest of the day in activities, reportedly with strong results that still need careful checking. Teachers need a decent salary floor and higher status; AI's pressure on tech jobs may push engineers into teaching. Source so far: one politician's campaign vision.

## Key ideas
- A personal AI tutor per child, mapping the curriculum and exams to that child's specific situation ([[episodes/tsarot-baheitek--90-naftali-bennett]], [[people/naftali-bennett]]).
- The teacher becomes a mentor and educator: personality, values, outdoor trips, the parts beyond AI ([[episodes/tsarot-baheitek--90-naftali-bennett]], [[people/naftali-bennett]]).
- Alpha School (a US school network): ~2 hours of morning learning with AI systems, the rest of the day outside; "looks very successful, but always check in depth", so adopt cautiously ([[episodes/tsarot-baheitek--90-naftali-bennett]], [[people/naftali-bennett]]).
- Teacher pay needn't match tech, but needs a dignified minimum; the AI jobs shock may bring engineers into teaching, which he sees as a blessing ([[episodes/tsarot-baheitek--90-naftali-bennett]], [[people/naftali-bennett]]).
- Ship it in short iterations: set the target, check progress every two weeks ([[episodes/tsarot-baheitek--90-naftali-bennett]], [[people/naftali-bennett]]).

## Disagreements & open questions
- Does the Alpha School result hold up under scrutiny, and does it generalize beyond a selective network? Bennett himself flags it as unverified ([[episodes/tsarot-baheitek--90-naftali-bennett]]).
