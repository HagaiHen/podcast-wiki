# Defense Tech

**Summary:** Israel is pitched as a natural defense-tech hub: global demand for defense is urgent, the country is a startup nation, and recent wars have touched nearly every form of combat (guerrilla warfare, dense urban fighting, drones, tunnels, long-range strikes, ballistic and cruise missiles, rockets). The proposed state role isn't subsidies but being a design partner and first customer: a fast track for startups selling cheap, fast-iterating products ($200, not the $50M systems of the large defense contractors), since validation by the army or intelligence agencies makes everyone else buy. Strategically, owning critical IP makes a country "essential" to allies rather than merely liked. These are campaign claims by one politician. A neighboring field, public safety, is being rebuilt along similar lines: 911 systems that stayed analog and on-premise for decades are moving to the cloud with location and video from the caller, AI agents answer calls when there aren't enough dispatchers, and drones are dispatched autonomously. October 7 is cited as the moment public safety became part of national security, and security remains Israel's strongest brand abroad.

## Key ideas
- Why Israel: urgent global demand, startup culture, and battle experience across every form of combat; "product-market fit 100%" ([[episodes/tsarot-baheitek--90-naftali-bennett]], [[people/naftali-bennett]]).
- The government as design partner and first customer, not investor: the defense ministry "open for business" to Israeli startups, with a fast track for fast products ([[episodes/tsarot-baheitek--90-naftali-bennett]], [[people/naftali-bennett]]).
- Cheap products at startup speed, many of them, versus the three big contractors (IAI, Elbit, Rafael) where each product is ~$50M ([[episodes/tsarot-baheitek--90-naftali-bennett]], [[people/naftali-bennett]]).
- Validation by the army or Mossad works as a sales reference: "if they use it, everyone will buy" ([[episodes/tsarot-baheitek--90-naftali-bennett]], [[people/naftali-bennett]]).
- "Essentiality": be needed, not loved. Bennett says he declined a US R&D partnership on the Iron Beam laser to keep the IP Israeli, so others now need Israel ([[episodes/tsarot-baheitek--90-naftali-bennett]], [[people/naftali-bennett]]).
- Sovereign defense AI on open-source models in isolated data centers ([[episodes/micha-stocks--naftali-bennett-economic-interview]], [[people/naftali-bennett]]).
- Emergency calls barely changed for ~75 years: voice only, analog lines, everything on-premise. An FCC figure he cites: ~10,000 people a year die in the US because responders can't locate them ([[episodes/yasmins-living-room--amir-elichai-carbyne]], [[people/amir-elichai]]).
- 911 in the cloud has to reach five nines (~2 minutes of downtime a year), since a dropped call can cost a life. Carbyne says it was first to do it, which is why product-market fit took about four years ([[episodes/yasmins-living-room--amir-elichai-carbyne]], [[people/amir-elichai]]).
- Automation: young Americans won't take ~$50k/year dispatcher jobs, so AI agents now answer 911 calls, many calls to one agent with no waiting time, alongside automatic dispatch and live translation ([[episodes/yasmins-living-room--amir-elichai-carbyne]], [[people/amir-elichai]]).
- Prevention: big data to predict incidents and pre-position forces, and drones launched autonomously. For a domestic-violence call in a New York high-rise, the call is transcribed, the floor is known, and a drone is at the window, so dispatch time becomes "negative" ([[episodes/yasmins-living-room--amir-elichai-carbyne]], [[people/amir-elichai]]).
- Robots instead of officers for dangerous entries (tunnels, armed incidents), which he discussed with Andy Rubin, now building robotics operating systems ([[episodes/yasmins-living-room--amir-elichai-carbyne]], [[people/amir-elichai]]).
- Public safety is now part of national security; at Axon he builds national public-safety modernization plans with heads of state. He says Carbyne-style situational awareness could have saved some lives on October 7, when callers were asked where they were ([[episodes/yasmins-living-room--amir-elichai-carbyne]], [[people/amir-elichai]]).
- Security tech is Israel's strongest brand abroad: Emirati and American contacts were confused by October 7, and the pager operation restored that image ([[episodes/yasmins-living-room--amir-elichai-carbyne]], [[people/amir-elichai]]).
