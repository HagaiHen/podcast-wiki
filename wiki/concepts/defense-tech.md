# Defense Tech

**Summary:** Israel is pitched as a natural defense-tech hub: global demand for defense is urgent, the country is a startup nation, and recent wars have touched nearly every form of combat (guerrilla warfare, dense urban fighting, drones, tunnels, long-range strikes, ballistic and cruise missiles, rockets). The proposed state role isn't subsidies but being a design partner and first customer: a fast track for startups selling cheap, fast-iterating products ($200, not the $50M systems of the large defense contractors), since validation by the army or intelligence agencies makes everyone else buy. Strategically, owning critical IP makes a country "essential" to allies rather than merely liked. These are campaign claims by one politician.

## Key ideas
- Why Israel: urgent global demand, startup culture, and battle experience across every form of combat; "product-market fit 100%" ([[episodes/tsarot-baheitek--90-naftali-bennett]], [[people/naftali-bennett]]).
- The government as design partner and first customer, not investor: the defense ministry "open for business" to Israeli startups, with a fast track for fast products ([[episodes/tsarot-baheitek--90-naftali-bennett]], [[people/naftali-bennett]]).
- Cheap products at startup speed, many of them, versus the three big contractors (IAI, Elbit, Rafael) where each product is ~$50M ([[episodes/tsarot-baheitek--90-naftali-bennett]], [[people/naftali-bennett]]).
- Validation by the army or Mossad works as a sales reference: "if they use it, everyone will buy" ([[episodes/tsarot-baheitek--90-naftali-bennett]], [[people/naftali-bennett]]).
- "Essentiality": be needed, not loved. Bennett says he declined a US R&D partnership on the Iron Beam laser to keep the IP Israeli, so others now need Israel ([[episodes/tsarot-baheitek--90-naftali-bennett]], [[people/naftali-bennett]]).
- Sovereign defense AI on open-source models in isolated data centers ([[episodes/micha-stocks--naftali-bennett-economic-interview]], [[people/naftali-bennett]]).
