# Leveraged ETFs

**Summary:** Funds that deliver a multiple (x2, x3, x5) of an index's or sector's move. They look great on social media in a rising market, but a x5 fund is wiped out by a 20% drop in the underlying, and in a normal 50% crash a leveraged fund goes to roughly zero. "Leverage the index, since indexes win long term" fails because you lose control over when you exit.

## Key ideas
- A x5 chip ETF up 10% in a week means +50% for you: very shareable. But a 20% fall erases the whole investment ([[episodes/lazy-investing--108-the-leverage-trap]], [[people/tamir]]).
- Expect ~50% index drops about once a decade historically; with leveraged funds, expect ~80% drops several times a decade, possibly total loss ([[episodes/lazy-investing--108-the-leverage-trap]]).
- Daily-reset leverage adds further decay (mentioned, not explained) ([[episodes/lazy-investing--108-the-leverage-trap]]).
