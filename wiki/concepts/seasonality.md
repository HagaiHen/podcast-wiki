# Seasonality (calendar effects like "Uptober")

**Summary:** Seasonality is the habit of reading calendar statistics as signals: Bitcoin rose in October in 11 of 16 years (2010–2025), averaging ~+25%, hence the nickname "Uptober". Fifteen years of data feels strong, but the asset doesn't know what month it is; moves come from sentiment, and the same October statistic says almost nothing for Ethereum, whose October has no link to how its year ends. A season that breaks its pattern can be read as either a coming catch-up or a sign the pattern was never causal.

## Key ideas
- Bitcoin in October, 2010–2025: positive in 11 of 16 years, average ~+25%, median ~12.6%, a ~68% positive rate; ~81% over 2015–2025 alone ([[episodes/micha-stocks--bitcoin-uptober]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- Ethereum's October is mixed (average ~+5%) and doesn't predict its year: green October with a red year and vice versa ([[episodes/micha-stocks--bitcoin-uptober]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- "These assets don't relate to the calendar"; what moves them is sentiment ([[episodes/micha-stocks--bitcoin-uptober]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).
- Sample size matters: shrinking the window from 16 years to 10 moved the positive rate from ~68% to ~81%, a reminder of how fragile such numbers are ([[episodes/micha-stocks--bitcoin-uptober]], [[people/micha-stocks]]).

## Disagreements & open questions
- If the first half of October is negative against a strong record, is that a setup for a strong second half or evidence the pattern isn't real? Micha leans optimistic but admits nobody knows ([[episodes/micha-stocks--bitcoin-uptober]]).

## Takeaways
- [ ] Don't treat a calendar statistic (e.g. "Uptober") as a buy signal by itself ([[episodes/micha-stocks--bitcoin-uptober]])
