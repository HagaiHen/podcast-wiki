# Customer Pain Research

**Summary:** The best small-business ideas hide in work customers still do by hand around software they already pay for. Agents with social-web access (Agent Reach for YouTube, Reddit and X) can mine posts for workaround phrases like "I export it" or "does anyone know a workaround" and bring back the original posts. The same agents can run as a standing trend monitor over your niches, so you build first while few others have. Research only produces hypotheses: you still talk to the people posting before assuming there's a customer. An interviewing skill like Grill Me helps you list the assumptions to take into that conversation. The pre-AI version is the same loop: Carbyne began when its founder called the police during a beach robbery and was asked to dictate his location, and he then talked to the police, the ambulance service and their vendors before building. The biggest founder mistake is a solution for a problem nobody has.

## Key ideas
- Ask an agent: for software people already pay for, what are customers still doing by hand? Bring back the original posts ([[episodes/startup-ideas--8-github-repos-viral-money]], [[people/greg-isenberg]]).
- Search phrases that signal workarounds: "I export it", "I copy this into", "does anyone know a workaround". Collect a few recent examples from different people ([[episodes/startup-ideas--8-github-repos-viral-money]], [[people/greg-isenberg]]).
- Plain LLMs are weak at this, so tools like Agent Reach set up access to social sites for cross-source research without manual collection ([[episodes/startup-ideas--8-github-repos-viral-money]], [[people/greg-isenberg]]).
- Research isn't validation: ask one poster to show what they actually do. The skill helps you prepare; the customer tells you what's happening ([[episodes/startup-ideas--8-github-repos-viral-money]], [[people/greg-isenberg]]).
- Surface hidden buyer questions early: for local franchise ads, who pays (owner or HQ) and who approves? You don't want to build a dashboard and then learn every ad goes through head office ([[episodes/startup-ideas--8-github-repos-viral-money]], [[people/greg-isenberg]]).
- The same research improves positioning for an existing product: customers' own complaint wording becomes copy ([[episodes/startup-ideas--8-github-repos-viral-money]], [[people/greg-isenberg]]).
- Trend machines: use ideabrowser.com or a personal agent platform (GrokBot, Claude) to watch micro-niches for emerging trends; it's surprising how few people have built anything when you're early ([[episodes/startup-ideas--8-ai-proof-businesses-2027]], [[people/greg-isenberg]]).
- Start from a problem you lived: being asked "where are you?" by the police in the 21st century led to an app that streams location and video to dispatch ([[episodes/yasmins-living-room--amir-elichai-carbyne]], [[people/amir-elichai]]).
- Validate with the buyers: police, Magen David Adom, emergency services and the companies selling to them. "The biggest mistake founders make is thinking they found a solution when nobody has the problem" ([[episodes/yasmins-living-room--amir-elichai-carbyne]], [[people/yasmin]]).
- AI tools like Claude and ChatGPT have shortened the path from zero to knowing whether you can do something ([[episodes/yasmins-living-room--amir-elichai-carbyne]], [[people/amir-elichai]]).

## Takeaways
- [ ] Have an agent search Reddit/X/YouTube for "I export it" / "workaround" posts about a paid tool and bring back originals ([[episodes/startup-ideas--8-github-repos-viral-money]])
